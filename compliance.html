hako is still in testing. Before the first boxes ship, this page will list its certifications (FCC in the US, ISED in Canada, CE and UKCA in Europe and the UK) and link to its declarations of conformity. The booklet in the box points here, so this is where the full statements will always live.

The device

Model: hako. Power: 5V ⎓ 3A over USB-C. hako connects over 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi through the Raspberry Pi computer inside it, and has an NFC tag under the top of the box that a phone can read.

Safety

Use the power supply that came with hako, or another certified USB-C supply. Indoors only; keep it away from water, heat and direct sun. Don't open the case: there are no user-serviceable parts inside. Not a toy.

Recycling

Don't put hako in household waste. Take it to an electronics collection point, or email us and we'll take it back and recycle it.
