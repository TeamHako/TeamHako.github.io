Compliance

hako is still in testing. Before the first boxes ship, this page will list its own declarations (FCC in the US and ISED in Canada first; Europe and the UK later). The booklet in the box points here, so this is where the full statements will always live.

The device

Model: hako. Power: 5V ⎓ 3A over USB-C. hako connects over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth LE through the Espressif ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 module inside it, and has an NFC tag under the top of the box that a phone can read.

Radio

hako's Wi-Fi is a certified radio module, the Espressif ESP32-S3-WROOM-1: Contains FCC ID: 2AC7Z-ESPS3WROOM1 · Contains IC: 21098-ESPS3WROOM1. hako as a whole is being tested now; its own FCC and ISED (Canada) declarations will appear here once the results are in.

Place and use hako at least 20 cm (8 in) from your body. Changes or modifications not expressly approved by the party responsible for compliance could void your authority to operate it.

Radio approvals held by Espressif for the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (certificates at espressif.com):

  • USA: FCC ID 2AC7Z-ESPS3WROOM1
  • Canada: IC 21098-ESPS3WROOM1
  • EU: EU type-examination certificate under the Radio Equipment Directive (notified body 0980)
  • Japan: R 201-220052
  • Brazil: ANATEL certificate of conformity

Safety

Use the power supply that came with hako, or another certified USB-C supply. Indoors only; keep it away from water, heat and direct sun. Don't open the case: there are no user-serviceable parts inside. Not a toy.

Recycling

Don't put hako in household waste. Take it to an electronics collection point, or email us and we'll take it back and recycle it.
