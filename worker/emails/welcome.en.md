Hi, I'm Jared.

Thanks for joining. I'm building hako by hand: a small walnut box with a 64×64
LED screen that sits on your desk and shows your Anki reviews. Turn the knob
to see what's due, tap your phone on top to open its settings, and watch a
little pet relax as your queue shrinks.

**What happens next**

  mark burned into the wood. You'll hear the day pre-orders open.

  hear when it launches.
