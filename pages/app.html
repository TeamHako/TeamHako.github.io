設 Installing

Two minutes, start to finish.

1 · Anki
Install the hako add-on: in Anki choose Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons and enter 1077082455, or double-click hako.ankiaddon. Restart Anki. AnkiConnect works too if you already use it.
Windows
Open the installer, hako-windows-setup.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info → Run anyway to continue.
Mac
Open the disk image (.dmg) and drag hako into Applications. The first time, macOS won't open it: in System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway to start it.
Linux
In a terminal, unpack it: tar -xzf hako-linux-x64.tar.gz. Then, in the new folder, run ./install.sh and hako shows up in your app menu.
2 · Open hako
With Anki open, hako finds your collection by itself. If the add-on asks, click Allow. Right-click the window for settings: screens, your study friend, the clock, always on top and starting at login.

Have a hako too? Both read the same Anki history, so a friend you unlock on one moves in on the other. Questions or a bug? Write to hello@hakoshop.com
