四 Process

Built by one person, in small batches.

手作り

Handmade enclosure

I cut each box from solid hardwood, join it by hand without screws or nails, and finish it with oil.

電子

Real components

A 64×64 RGB LED panel, a knob, a shake sensor and an NFC tag for tap-to-connect, on one custom circuit board with no loose wires. Every unit is burned in and self-tested before it ships.

少量

Small batches, by design

The first batch is 25 because that's how many I can build well by hand. After that, a bigger run goes to Kickstarter.

What's inside the box

It has one knob, a shake sensor, and an NFC chip under the top of the box that you tap your phone on.

  1. 64×64 LED panel 4,096 lights, easy to read from across the room
  2. Raspberry Pi and matrix driver runs hako's software and updates itself overnight
  3. NFC chip, just under the top tap your phone on the top of the box to set up Wi-Fi or open hako's web page
  4. Shake sensor a gentle shake shows the next screen
  5. Knob turn to change screens, press to refresh, hold for the menu
  6. Power one adapter, made for your country
五 Maker

Made by Jared.

I'm a Japanese learner and YouTuber who's spent the last few years documenting AJATT-style immersion at @jareddesu. hako started because my desk needed something quieter than a phone and more present than a tab.

It's a solo project. Every unit will be assembled, tested, and shipped by me. If you have a question, you'll hear back from me directly.
