箱The device

Meet hako.

A walnut box with a 64×64 LED screen that shows your Anki reviews, your streak and a small friend who keeps you company. Here's how it works, who lives in it, and how to get one.

友 Friends

Keep studying. New friends move in.

Every hako starts with one study buddy. Hit a milestone and another one moves in, with its own face, its own moods and a few hundred little reactions. They blink, look around, sneeze, nod off at night and cheer when your reviews hit zero.

A little party for every milestone.

When you earn a new friend, hako throws confetti and introduces them. Your pet reacts to your day as well: a bounce for each review, a cheer when you finish, a double take when a pile of cards comes due, a yawn on the first look of the morning. Give the box a shake and see what happens.

二 How it works

Setup takes about five minutes.

You don't need an app or an account, and your study data stays on your Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Plug it in

    Snap the stand onto the back and plug it in. hako opens its own setup network and shows a QR code.

  2. 2

    Connect with your phone

    Scan the code, or tap your phone on the top of the box. Pick your Wi-Fi and type the password. Time zone and language are set for you.

  3. 3

    Add the hako add-on to Anki

    In Anki, Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, code 1077082455. Click Allow when hako asks.

  4. 4

    Study as usual

    Your reviews show up on the box while Anki is open. Updates install themselves overnight.
