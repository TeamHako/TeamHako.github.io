一Setup

Setup takes about five minutes.

  1. Plug it in. Use the USB-C power supply from the box (5V ⎓ 3A). After a few seconds the screen says join wifi hako-XXXX and shows a QR code. The four letters after hako- are your hako's own.
  2. Connect from your phone. Scan the code, or join hako-XXXX in your phone's Wi-Fi settings. On Android you can also just tap your phone on the top of hako. A setup page opens by itself: pick your home Wi-Fi and type its password. hako uses 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi; almost every home router has it.
  3. Add the hako add-on to Anki. On the computer you study on, open Anki and go to Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons…, enter the code 1077082455 and restart Anki. When Anki asks whether to allow your hako, click Allow. hako can read your decks and reviews, but never changes them.
  4. Study as usual. Keep Anki open on the same Wi-Fi as hako. It finds Anki by itself within a minute and keeps up from then on.
Studying on more than one computer? hako talks to whichever one has Anki open, as long as the add-on is installed on each.

二The knob

One knob does everything.

TurnSwitch screens. In a menu, move through it.
PressRefresh from Anki. In a menu, choose.
Double-press, or holdOpen the menu. In a game, a double-press pauses or leaves it.
Hold for 3 secondsSleep: the screen goes dark. Press or turn to wake it.

三Screens

Seven screens, all optional.

TodayCards due today, about how long they'll take, and a bar that fills as you go.
HealthYour collection by state: mature, young, learning, new and suspended.
HeatmapThe last six weeks of study, one square a day, with your streak underneath. The color is yours to choose on the settings page.
PetYour study friend (see Pets).
CardsYour recent cards, random ones, or cards with a tag you pick, with an optional reading underneath, like the kana for a kanji.
RetentionHow many reviews you got right over the last day, week and month.
ClockThe time and the weather for your city.

Hide the screens you don't want, and choose the cards, the reading field and the fonts, on the settings page.

The corner dot

The little dot in the top-right corner shows how hako is getting on with Anki.

GreenConnected: Anki answered the last check.
Pulsing tealChecking with Anki right now. hako checks every 30 seconds.
Blinking yellowAnki hasn't answered for over a minute. hako keeps trying.
OrangeNo answer for 5 minutes. The numbers are from the last sync.
Redhako hasn't reached Anki yet.
Pink pixel in the very cornerAnother hako is nearby.

四Pets

Keep studying. New friends move in.

Every hako starts with one study friend. Others move in as you study:

ChibiFrom day one.
MochiA 7-day streak.
Onigiri1,000 reviews.
KaoA 30-day streak.

When a friend moves in, hako throws a little party and introduces them. Once they're in, they stay, even if a streak breaks. Switch pets with the knob menu's Pet item or on the settings page.

Pets react to your day: a bounce for each review, a cheer when you finish, a double take when a pile of cards comes due. They doze off at night. Give the box a shake while one is on screen. If another hako is on the same Wi-Fi nearby, a pink dot lights up in the corner and your pet perks up.

五Games

Four games in the knob menu.

Open the menu, choose Games, then pick one. A double-press leaves Pong, Pegs and Quiz; in Liquid it opens the pause menu.

Pong

Turn the knob to move your paddle; press to start. First to the winning score takes it.

Pegs

Turn to aim, press to drop a ball. It lights every peg it touches; clear all the orange pegs before your ten balls run out. Tougher pegs take two or three hits. Land a ball in the moving bucket, or light a lot of pegs in one shot, for a free ball. Clear a board and the next one is new.

Liquid

Tilt hako and the water flows. Shake it to splash, turn the knob to fill or drain, and press to switch between water, lava and slime. A double-press opens its pause menu: Resume, Calibrate or Exit.

If the water flows the wrong way, choose Calibrate and follow the pictures: stand hako upright and press, lay it on its left side (the knob pointing up) and press, then lay it face down and press. A green tick means it's done; hako remembers it.

Quiz

A card from your deck at the top, four readings underneath: turn to pick, press to answer. Quiz appears once hako knows a reading field for your cards (set it on the settings page under Cards) and has at least four different readings to choose from.

六Settings

The settings page.

hako has its own settings page, on your phone or computer, while it's on the same Wi-Fi:

  • Tap your phone on the top of hako (the NFC tag is under the wood there), or
  • open hako.local in a browser, or
  • open the knob menu → Info and type the address shown there, or scan its code.

The first time a browser opens it, hako shows a six-digit code on its screen: type it in. That browser stays paired; a phone tap pairs it by itself.

NowToday's numbers, your collection, a live view of the screen and connecting to Anki.
SettingsWhich screens to show; the cards (recent, random or a tag), their field, the reading under them and the fonts; the heatmap color; your pet; your city for the weather and time zone, 12- or 24-hour time and units; the sleep schedule and dimming at night; brightness; shake to switch, hakos nearby, screen orientation and knob direction; switching screens by themselves; language.
AdvancedAnki's address if hako can't find it by itself, a debug summary and the factory reset.
HelpThe knob in short, quick fixes, travel tips, the desktop app and an email button with your hako's details filled in.

七Sleep

Sleep and night.

  • Sleep now: hold the knob for three seconds, or use the button on the settings page. Press or turn the knob to wake hako; a shake wakes it too unless you turn that off.
  • Sleep schedule: on the settings page, add up to six times of day for the screen to turn off, like the night or a meeting. Press the knob to peek; hako dozes off again after a minute.
  • Dim at night: instead of turning off, the screen dims from sunset to sunrise where your weather city is (or 8 pm to 7 am without one). Pick how dim with the slider under it.

八Wi-Fi

Wi-Fi and travel.

hako remembers every Wi-Fi it joins, and which computer Anki was on for each one, so coming home needs nothing.

A friend's place or a new home

Plug hako in. Away from your saved Wi-Fi it keeps showing your last stats and never starts setup by itself. To join a new Wi-Fi, open the knob menu → WiFi → Setup; a press of the knob cancels it. Join hako-XXXX on your phone, pick the Wi-Fi and type the password. Open Anki on your laptop on the same Wi-Fi and hako finds it.

A café, library or hotel

Public Wi-Fi often keeps devices apart, or needs a sign-in page that hako can't get past. Use a hotspot instead: turn on your phone's hotspot and connect your laptop to it. Then start hako's setup, join hako-XXXX from the laptop and pick your phone's hotspot. On Windows the laptop itself can be the hotspot (Settings → Mobile hotspot). hako remembers it for next time.

Switching between saved Wi-Fi

Knob menu → WiFi → Switch lists the networks in range, saved ones first. Pick one to hop over without the setup page.

No internet at all

hako keeps showing the clock, your pet, the games and your numbers from the last sync, even after you unplug it. The weather and Anki numbers catch up once it's back online.

九Updates

Updates install themselves.

New screens, pets, games and fixes arrive overnight over Wi-Fi. Every update is signed, so hako only installs ones that really come from us. If an update ever fails to start, hako goes back to the version before by itself. The settings page shows your version and lets you check for an update or install one now.

十Fixes

If something's not right.

Open Anki on your computer and check the hako add-on is installed (Tools → Add-ons). hako and the computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi. Then press the knob once to refresh. Still nothing? On the settings page, Now → Look for Anki now, or type Anki's address under Advanced.

hako can only reach Anki while Anki is open on a computer on the same Wi-Fi. If the dot blinks yellow a lot, the computer may be going to sleep, its Wi-Fi may be saving power, or Anki may be busy syncing. The settings page shows the last error under Advanced → Anki connection. Closing Anki is fine: the dot turns orange and hako keeps your last numbers.

Stay joined to hako-XXXX and open any website, like example.com: your phone should switch to hako's page. If your phone says the network has no internet, choose to stay connected. You can also type 192.168.4.1 in the browser.

Your phone or computer must be on the same Wi-Fi as hako. If hako.local doesn't work, open the knob menu → Info and type the address shown there.

Use the USB-C power supply that came with hako (5V, 3A): weaker phone chargers can't power the full panel, so hako turns itself down. Also check dim at night and the brightness on the settings page.

Knob menu → Rotate turns the picture 90° at a time, and Flip knob reverses the knob. Both are on the settings page too.

Double-press in the game, choose Calibrate and follow the three pictures (see Games).

Quiz needs a reading field for your cards: set reading under the card on the settings page, and have at least four cards with different readings.

If something ever freezes, hako restarts itself and carries on. The settings page counts these under the version number, with the reason. If the count keeps growing, email us and include it.

十一Reset

Factory reset.

A factory reset forgets your Wi-Fi, the Anki connection, paired browsers, earned pets and every setting, then restarts hako in setup mode, as it came out of the box. Its serial number and calibration stay.

  • With the knob: unplug hako, then hold the knob down while you plug it back in. The screen counts down from 10. Keep holding until it says Erasing; let go any time before that to cancel.
  • On the settings page: Advanced → Factory reset, then confirm.

Afterwards, set hako up again as in Setup.

十二Care

Care and help.

  • Wipe hako with the cloth from the box, dry. Once or twice a year, rub in a little food-safe wood oil.
  • Keep it out of direct sun and away from water and heat. Indoor use only.
  • Use the included power supply, or another certified USB-C supply. Don't open the case: there are no user-serviceable parts inside.
  • Warranty: two years. If it stops working, we'll repair or replace it. After that, we'll repair it for the cost of parts and shipping. Returns within 30 days of delivery.

Stuck, or something not working? Email hello@hakoshop.com. The settings page's Help tab has a button that fills in your hako's details for you. Regulatory information is on the compliance page.
