Open-source licenses

Last updated October 2026

hako's software is built on free and open-source work. Thank you to everyone below. Each component keeps its own license; the full texts come with the software on the device and are available on request.

On the device

  • Raspberry Pi OS (based on Debian): many free-software licenses, including the GNU GPL. Source: raspberrypi.com and debian.org.
  • rpi-rgb-led-matrix by Henner Zeller, which drives the LED panel: GNU GPL v2. We'll send you the complete corresponding source code, including our build settings, on request for at least three years after we last ship hako: email hello@hakoshop.com.
  • Python libraries: Flask and Werkzeug (BSD-3-Clause), Requests (Apache 2.0), Pillow (MIT-CMU), qrcode (BSD), cryptography (Apache 2.0 / BSD), NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), gpiozero (BSD-3-Clause), mpu6050-raspberrypi (MIT).
  • Alpine.js, used by the settings page: MIT.

Fonts on the screen

  • Misaki Gothic and k8x12 by Num Kadoma: free to use, copy and redistribute, including commercially.
  • GNU Unifont: SIL Open Font License 1.1, and GNU GPL v2+ with the font embedding exception.
  • Fusion Pixel by TakWolf: SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Printed materials

  • Zen Old Mincho and Zen Kaku Gothic New: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
