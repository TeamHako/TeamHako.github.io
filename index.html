一 What it is

A glanceable view of your review queue.

01

Today's load, at a glance

How many cards are due. How many you've done. Time remaining. One number, one color, no notification dread.

02

A study companion, not a coach

A small chibi mood-shifts with your workload. Behind on reviews, it looks worried. Caught up, it rests easy. No streaks, no shame.

03

Cards you've recently added

A rotating window into your newest additions, random samples, or anything tagged. Quiet revision while you work on other things.

04

Six weeks of history

A heatmap of your past reviews. Retention windows. Card health. Enough context to feel your progress without obsessing over it.

二 How it works

Plug it in. Five minutes later, it's yours.

No app to download, no account to make. Everything stays on your own Wi-Fi.

  1. 1

    Plug it in

    hako opens its own setup network and shows a QR code.

  2. 2

    Connect with your phone

    Scan the code, or tap your phone on the box. Pick your Wi-Fi and type the password. Time zone and language are set for you.

  3. 3

    Add the hako add-on to Anki

    In Anki, Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons, code 1077082455. Click Allow when hako asks.

  4. 4

    Study as usual

    Your reviews show up on the box while Anki is open. Updates install themselves overnight.

四 Process

Built by one person, in small batches.

手作り

Handmade enclosure

Each box is cut from solid hardwood and held together with Japanese joinery, fitted by hand in my workshop. No screws, no nails. A soft natural oil finish — just the wood.

電子

Real components

A Raspberry Pi 3 A+ drives a 64×64 RGB LED matrix, with a knob, a shake sensor and an NFC tag for tap-to-open. Every unit is burned in and self-tested before it ships.

少量

Small batches, by design

The first batch is 25 units. I'd rather make 25 well than 250 hastily. If you miss it, join the waitlist: a larger run on Kickstarter comes next.
